## Setup traps

- The PyPI `sglang-kernel` 0.4.10 wheel lags the tag (`moe.apply_gguf_moe_weights` is missing while the tag's
  `__init__` imports it). `setup_sgl_kernel.sh` now copies the tagged tree's top-level wrapper modules over the
  PyPI skeleton.
- `pyproject_other.toml` still pins `openai` 2.6.1 while `responses_tools/beam.py` needs 2.8.0. `setup.sh` pins
  `openai==2.8.0`, `tml-renderers==0.1.0` and `python-dateutil<3`.

- Accept length is identical across versions: qwen38 DSpark 3.695 (4,800 tokens / 1,299 verifies), Flint
  DFlash2 2.970 (4,800 / 1,616).
- qwen38 cold prefill: 2,587–2,598 tok/s at 51,761 tokens and 2,079–2,090 at 175,753 on both versions. Greedy
  needle 58K / 190K × 0.1 / 0.5 / 0.9 with a flush per cell: 6/6 on all four boots. Boot logs carry the same
  kernel "active" lines (one-pass HIP prefill attention in fp8 QK mode, BF16 attention-sum wire, HIP GEMV and
  GEMM, both split-KV verify stages), so v0.5.23's K/V halves of one buffer serve patches 082 and 131 unchanged.
- Flint DFlash2 cold prefill 6.5K / 3.55K tok/s on both versions. Its greedy filler needle is 3/3 at 58K and 0/3
  at 190K on both versions, cell for cell (reasoning loops to the token cap): a model behaviour, not the rebase.
  On v0.5.23 with default sampling and thinking on, a natural-text haystack is found in 16 of 18 cells
  (58K / 131K / 190K, 6 of 6 at 190K) and the filler haystack at 190K in 1 of 6: the loop is the low-entropy
  filler, not depth.
- Fleet pass (`fleet_validate.sh`, 18 presets with Flint added): every preset boots; the late needle and
  coherence check pass at true depth on 17, Flint's is truncated on both versions. Capability failures are the
  thinking probe on non-reasoning coder and Devstral presets and on qwen3vl-32b (same on v0.5.22), and
  gemma4-31b image input, which fails vision and video on v0.5.22 as well.
- The 3090 rig's nemotron-omni regression since v0.5.23 does not reproduce here (0.4–0.9 % lower).
